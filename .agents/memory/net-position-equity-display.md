---
name: Net Position equity display
description: Partner equity remains outside asset/liability totals but is shown separately in Net Position details.
---

Partner-capital accounts must remain typed as Equity and must not be folded into
What We Have or What We Owe. Net Position details may show their signed balances
in a separate Partner Capital / Equity section.

**Why:** Reclassifying partner capital as an asset or liability inflates or
misstates the balance-sheet totals and can break specialized Golden Coast
posting rules.

**How to apply:** Keep canonical Golden Coast types (`Equity` and `Loans`) in
the database. Update classification/presentation code when those accounts need
to be visible, and keep the displayed equity section excluded from the net
position formula.
