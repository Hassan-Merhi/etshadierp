# Use Cases

## For the Accountant

**What you'll use most:**
- Vouchers — record payments, receipts, and journal entries
- Accounts — view ledger balances and account statements
- Daybook — daily transaction summary with Dr/Cr breakdown
- Supplier & Customer statements — track what's owed and what's due

**Key tips:**
- Opening balances are entered as vouchers on your start date.
- Every voucher must balance (debits = credits) before it can be saved.
- Filter account statements by date range and export as PDF for audits.

---

## For the Warehouse / Inventory Manager

**What you'll use most:**
- Inventory — current stock levels per item per location
- Stock Transfers — move goods between locations
- Purchase Orders — receive goods from suppliers
- Containers — track incoming international shipments

**Key tips:**
- Mark a transfer "Completed" only when goods physically arrive — stock updates at that point.
- Container costs (freight, duty, commission) flow into per-kg cost of received bales.
- Use the barcode scanner on finalized bales for quick lookup.

---

## For the Factory Supervisor

**What you'll use most:**
- Bales Hub → Stock Entry — record daily bale production
- Bales Hub → Stock Entry History — review past entries by date/worker/product
- Workers — manage worker groups and production assignments
- Wipers Re-Entry — back-date wipers bale creation when needed

**Key tips:**
- Once a bale is created via stock entry, its assigned worker cannot be changed (locked).
- Bales must be finalized before they can be printed or scanned.
- Use the Bale Relabeling page to reassign or relabel bales before dispatch.

---

## For the Payroll Manager

**What you'll use most:**
- Employees — manage employee records, salaries, and bonuses
- Payroll → Advances — issue and track salary advances
- Payroll → Run Payroll — generate monthly salary payments
- Payroll → Bonus Calculator — compute sales % or bales-based bonuses

**Key tips:**
- Outstanding advances are auto-deducted during payroll — adjust the amount if doing partial recovery.
- Bonuses are calculated separately and added before running payroll.
- Each employee has an account in the ledger — their statement shows all salary and advance history.

---

## For the POS Cashier

**What you'll use most:**
- POS Terminal — process customer sales
- Daybook — view today's sales summary (if access is granted)

**Key tips:**
- You are locked to your assigned location — you can only sell from your shop's stock.
- Cost and profit columns are hidden from POS users by default.
- If an item doesn't appear, it may be out of stock at your location.

---

## For the System Admin

**What you'll use most:**
- Settings → Users — create accounts and set passwords
- Settings → User Access Management — grant/revoke page access per user
- Settings → Company — configure name, currency, and address
- Settings → Locations — add or edit locations

**Key tips:**
- Admin users always have full access and bypass page-level restrictions.
- Use the login history page to audit who accessed the system and when.
- Exchange rates can be updated daily in Settings → Exchange Rates.
