# Examples

## Example 1 — Record a Purchase and Update Stock

1. Go to **Purchasing → Purchase Orders** → New Order.
2. Select supplier, add items with quantities and unit costs.
3. Save and mark as **Received**.
4. Stock is automatically updated at the chosen location.
5. A payable voucher is created in Accounts for the supplier balance.

---

## Example 2 — Run Monthly Payroll for Workers

1. Go to **Payroll → Run Payroll**.
2. Select the month and choose **Workers** mode.
3. The preview pre-fills each worker's base salary and any outstanding advance deductions.
4. Adjust deductions if paying only a partial advance amount.
5. Click **Confirm Payroll** — a payment voucher is posted for each worker.

---

## Example 3 — Process a POS Sale

1. Go to **Sales & POS → POS Terminal**.
2. Search for items and add them to the cart.
3. Enter the customer (or leave as walk-in).
4. Select payment method and complete the sale.
5. The sale appears in the **Daybook** immediately.

---

## Example 4 — Issue a Salary Advance

1. Go to **Payroll → Advances → New Advance**.
2. Select the employee, enter the amount and date.
3. Save — the advance shows as outstanding on the employee's balance.
4. On the next payroll run, the advance is auto-filled as a deduction.
5. Once fully recovered, the advance is marked **Fully Paid** automatically.

---

## Example 5 — Transfer Stock Between Locations

1. Go to **Inventory → Stock Transfers → New Transfer**.
2. Select source location, destination location, and items with quantities.
3. Save as **Pending**, then mark **Completed** when goods physically move.
4. Stock decreases at source and increases at destination.

---

## Example 6 — View a Supplier Statement

1. Go to **Accounts** and search for the supplier's account.
2. Click the account name to open the statement dialog.
3. Filter by date range (This Month, Last Month, or custom).
4. Export as PDF or print directly from the dialog.

---

## Example 7 — Create a Factory Stock Entry (Bales)

1. Go to **Factory → Bales Hub → Stock Entry** tab.
2. Choose entry date, location, and worker.
3. Add products (wipers, rags, etc.) with quantity and weight.
4. Confirm — bales are created, finalized, and ready to scan or print labels.
